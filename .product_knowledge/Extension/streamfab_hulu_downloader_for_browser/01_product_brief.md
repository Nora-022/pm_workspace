# 01 Product Brief

## One-line Positioning
StreamFab Hulu Downloader for Browser is a Hulu plugin for selecting Hulu content from metadata-driven trees and downloading it in promoted high quality for offline viewing.

## Current Confirmed User Value
- download Hulu videos in up to `1080p/4K`
- save videos as `MP4`
- keep `EAC3 5.1` audio where supported by the product page promise
- select the correct Hulu content item from season/version-based trees

## Current Confirmed Scope
- Hulu-related site research has been started for:
  - `hulu.com`
  - `hulu.jp`
- StreamFab product-page facts have been extracted and recorded
- PM kickoff files and requirement working documents are initialized

## Current Non-goals
- assuming `.com` and `.jp` are operationally identical
- assuming audio/subtitle are configurable in the right panel
