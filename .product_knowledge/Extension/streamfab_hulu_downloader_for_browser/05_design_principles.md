# 05 Design Principles

## Hulu Kickoff Principles
- Prefer a content-tree-first UI.
- Keep version meaning explicit when subtitle-version or dubbed-version exists.
- Do not invent controls that the current Hulu plugin flow does not expose.
- Separate content identity from stream-quality identity.

## Copy Principles
- Banner copy can safely use product-page-backed capability wording such as `up to 4K`.
- Audio copy should only use `EAC3 5.1` where product-page-backed wording is appropriate.
- Do not overgeneralize `.com` vocabulary to `.jp`, or vice versa.

## Structure Principles
- Prefer the shallowest tree that still explains the content correctly.
- Treat `.com` and `.jp` as potentially different service surfaces until later convergence is proven.
