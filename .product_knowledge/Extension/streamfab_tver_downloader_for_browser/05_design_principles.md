# 05 Design Principles

## Principles
- Preserve the common StreamFab browser-plugin information architecture unless TVer requires a divergence.
- Keep all TVer-specific claims traceable to either client product-page evidence or site research.
- Prefer explicit "pending confirmation" markers over inherited assumptions.

## Initial UI Direction
- Brand as `StreamFab TVer Downloader for Browser`
- Prepare banner, installer assets, and store screenshots only after confirmed slogan and site imagery are available
