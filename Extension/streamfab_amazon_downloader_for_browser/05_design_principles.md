# 05 Design Principles

## Visual Direction
- Keep the established StreamFab browser-plugin layout and interaction patterns.
- Replace branding, screenshots, and wording with Amazon / Prime Video-specific assets only.

## Banner Direction
- Use product-page-backed messaging rather than speculative feature copy.
- Candidate EN line:
  - `Amazon Videos in Up to 1080p. One Click.`
- Candidate ZH line:
  - `Amazon 视频一键下载，轻松离线观看。`
- Candidate supporting line:
  - `Save Prime Video titles as MP4 or MKV with EAC3 5.1 / AAC 2.0 audio.`

## Asset Dependencies
- Amazon / Prime Video homepage or title-page screenshots for store materials
- plugin logo derived from the product naming convention
- a confirmed configuration-dialog screenshot once the plugin runs
- at least one movie detail page screenshot and one episodic detail page screenshot for store collateral

## Avoid
- claiming 4K or HDR without direct evidence
- claiming support for every Prime Video surface before validation
- using artwork that implies sports / live coverage before the scope is confirmed
