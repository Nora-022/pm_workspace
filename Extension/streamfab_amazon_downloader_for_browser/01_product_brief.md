# 01 Product Brief

## One-line Positioning
`StreamFab Amazon Downloader for Browser` is a single-site Prime Video downloader plugin for saving supported Amazon streaming titles for offline viewing in up to `1080p`.

## Current Scope
- Primary target site: `primevideo.com`
- Primary content focus: on-demand Prime Video movies and TV shows
- Product-page-backed capability line: `up to 1080p`, `EAC3 5.1 / AAC 2.0`, `MP4/MKV`, `H.264/H.265`
- Core user value: detect playable Amazon titles in-browser and send them into the StreamFab download workflow

## Non-goals For This Init
- Do not assume support for all Amazon storefront video surfaces.
- Do not assume support for `Live TV`, `Sports`, and all `Channels` on day one.
- Do not assume rental / purchase content follows the same entitlement and error logic until validated.

## Current Inputs
- Client product page provides resolution, audio, and pricing clues.
- Target-site homepage confirms a multi-surface Prime Video experience with sign-in and entitlement gating.
