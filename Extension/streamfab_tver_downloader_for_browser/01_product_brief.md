# 01 Product Brief

## One-line Positioning
StreamFab TVer Downloader for Browser is a TVer-focused browser plugin workspace for detecting playable TVer content and defining a site-specific download experience.

## Current Confirmed Scope
- TVer site support only
- Browser-plugin product positioning under StreamFab
- Requirement and UI work documents initialized

## Current Non-goals
- Claiming confirmed download quality, audio, subtitle, or DRM support before verification
- Reusing assumptions from other single-site plugins as TVer facts
- Treating provisional site notes as final product boundaries
