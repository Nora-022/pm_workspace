# RecordFab Product Research

## 1. Research Overview
- Product name: RecordFab
- Target service: Streaming recording product
- Research stage: First-pass public information collection
- Confidence level: Medium

## 2. One-page Summary
- What this product is:
  - A desktop product focused on recording streaming videos, live streams, online courses, and social media content.
- Why it matters:
  - It covers the “save content locally” scenario when direct downloading is unavailable or unstable.
- Core characteristics:
  - Integrated browser
  - Auto-detect video stream
  - 720p / 1080p
  - 1x-5x recording speed
  - MP4 local output
- Initial implications:
  - Product design should focus on the recording workflow, not downloader logic.

## 3. Confirmed Public Facts
- Supports recording streaming video, live stream, online course, and social media content.
- Supports 720p and 1080p.
- Supports recording speed from 1x to 5x.
- Some websites only support 1x.
- Saves output as MP4.
- Windows system requirements are publicly listed.

## 4. Major Unknowns
- Trial and pricing model
- Entitlement and authorization rules
- Recording task queue behavior
- Retry / history / pause capabilities
- Mac support or other platform plans

## 5. Implications for Product Knowledge
- `01_product_brief.md` can be treated as stable first-pass context.
- `06_business_rules.md` should be the next priority because it still has many unknowns.
- `03_page_structure.md` and `04_interaction_details.md` should remain “proposal-level” until product screenshots are available.
